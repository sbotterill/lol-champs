## When to Flex Irelia

Irelia is listed as an alt in 28 matchups. Consider picking her over the default when:
- Your team lacks AD damage
- Enemy team has low CC (Irelia thrives when she can freely reset)
- You need a split-push threat

Her best alt matchups (biggest WR over the default): Smolder (68.8%), Naafiri (57.0%), Taliyah (55.2%), Yone (54.7%), Syndra (54.6%), Corki (54.5%), Zed (54.1%), Annie (54.1%)
